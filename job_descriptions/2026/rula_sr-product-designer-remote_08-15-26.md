## Full Job Description

We believe that mental health is just as important as physical health. We recognize that mental health issues can be complex and multifaceted, and we are dedicated to treating the whole person, not just the symptoms.

We aim to create a world where mental health is no longer stigmatized or marginalized, but rather is embraced as an integral part of one's overall well-being. 

We believe that by providing quality care that is both evidence-based and compassionate, we can empower individuals to take charge of their mental health and achieve their full potential. We are passionate about making a positive impact on the lives of those struggling with mental health issues and we strive to be a force for positive change in the field of mental healthcare.

Rula is a remote-first company. We currently hire in most U.S. states, with the exception of Hawaii.

**About the Role**

We're seeking a Sr. Product Designer to join Rula's Provider Team—a group dedicated to helping providers deliver exceptional mental health care. You'll design the tools therapists use every day, streamlining administrative work and building intelligent experiences that help them make informed clinical decisions, focusing on what matters most: their patients.

This role is ideal for a designer who thrives in ambiguity, enjoys taking 0→1 products from concept to execution, and brings a high level of ownership to every stage of the design process. You'll be expected to proactively identify opportunities, think strategically about the future of care delivery, and deliver polished, thoughtfully crafted experiences that leverage AI strategically in close partnership with Product, Engineering, Clinical, and Research.

**Required Qualifications**

- 5+ years of professional experience in Product Design with a strong portfolio demonstrating shipped products.
- Demonstrated success designing and optimizing multi-step workflows for productivity tools, operations platforms, or business software, with a clear track record of reducing task friction and driving measurable gains in workflow efficiency.
- Experience collaborating cross-functionally with product, engineering, operations, and (ideally) compliance or policy partners.
- Mastery of Figma for end-to-end product design, prototyping, and system-level thinking.
- Strong communication skills; able to articulate tradeoffs clearly and document workflow logic for cross-functional alignment.
- Ownership orientation with extensive experience owning product design from problem space to feature delivery and evolution

**Preferred Qualifications**

*While having the preferred qualifications enhances your candidacy, having all of them is not mandatory. We encourage all interested applicants to apply, even those who may not meet every preferred requirement.*

- Experience working in high-trust, platform domains including healthcare, fintech, and enterprise systems
- Experience designing for business or practice management teams with dynamic, complex workflows.
- Comfort translating ambiguous problems into lightweight, AI-enabled experiences
- Experience conducting user research with business or practice owners to uncover workflow friction.
- Experience contributing to or building enterprise-grade design systems
- Familiarity with accessibility requirements for finance/healthcare experiences
