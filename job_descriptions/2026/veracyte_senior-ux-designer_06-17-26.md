The Position:
The Senior UX Designer is an individual contributor role focused on creating intuitive, scalable, and user-centered experiences across Veracyte’s internal systems and customer-facing portals. This role partners closely with Product, Engineering, and Operations to translate complex diagnostic workflows into seamless digital experiences that drive usability, efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

As a strong design thinker, this individual applies deep subject matter expertise to solve complex challenges, exercises independent judgment, and collaborates effectively with cross-functional stakeholders to achieve business and user goals. The role requires expertise in multi-persona design, experience within Salesforce ecosystems, and proficiency with modern design tools such as the Adobe Creative Suite and Figma.

Location: This is a hybrid position based in our San Diego or South San Francisco office.&nbsp;&nbsp;Based on candidate location, we may consider U.S. remote.

Key Responsibilities

Design end-to-end user experiences across portal and internal workflows, ensuring consistency across multiple products and personas (providers, lab operations, internal users)

Transform report designs from the Adobe Creative suite into responsive HTML/CSS templates for automated report generation, partnering with engineering and QA teams to support Salesforce integration, dynamic data population, rendering validation, and ongoing template optimization.

Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs that aligned to brand guidelines using Figma

Collaborate with Product Marketing, Customer Care and Engineering to translate complex workflows (e.g., ordering, sample tracking, billing) into intuitive interfaces within Salesforce environments

Conduct user research, usability testing, and persona development to inform design decisions

Define and maintain reusable design components and contribute to design systems

Document user flows, interaction patterns, and design specifications for development and QA

Analyze user feedback and data to continuously improve usability and experience quality

Lead UX design efforts for complex projects and represent the UX function across project phases

Ability to design immersive, differentiated experiences for modern UI frameworks (e.g., React), supporting both front-end and full-stack development.
