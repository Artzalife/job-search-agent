We’re looking for a creative and driven Product Designer II to join our Pinner Actionability team and help shape the future of how people realize inspiration and plan with Pinterest. In this role, you’ll focus on transforming boards; one of our core features where people visualize, evaluate, and dream. You’ll have the chance to push boundaries, working hands-on with product and engineering partners to launch fast experiments, build bold new ideas, and continually refine the user experience.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

What you’ll do:

Design and ship UI for login and sign up and conversion upsells flows, supporting the overall Pinner activation journey.

Collaborate closely with product managers, engineers, and data partners to define and execute on roadmaps for the Pinner Conversion and Identity teams.

Be comfortable in a fast paced, iterative, experimental, and data driven environment

Work with and manage feedback from multiple stakeholders across Growth and Core teams, as well as engineering, product and design leadership

Uphold and inspire a high level of design execution throughout the team

&nbsp;

What we’re looking for:

2+ years of experience designing digital products.

Experience designing for complex products and solving for both user needs and business metrics, driving impact through both 0 to 1 product development and optimizations.

A portfolio demonstrating strong product and visual design skills, including solid understanding of layout, typography, color, imagery, and other design fundamentals.

Has a learner and builder mindset: Able to quickly onboard to emerging AI tools and implement them appropriately in the full product development process with a focus on decision quality, craft and efficiency.

Expertise in applying systems, frameworks, and standards, including using AI-powered tools and workflows to help scale your approach and facilitates productive cross-functional collaboration.

Sense of ownership, attention to detail, and a track record of shipping high-quality digital products.

Ability to think holistically and create scalable systems for experimentation and iteration, including designing for mobile and desktop platforms.

Excellent product thinking, UX strategy, and problem-solving skills.

Great communications skills with the ability to articulate your design decisions clearly and bring cross-functional partners along for the journey.

Comfortable balancing speed and quality in a fast-paced, data-driven, and iterative environment.

High level of self-awareness with a growth mindset that is focused on impact.

Bachelor’s degree in design, a related field or equivalent experience.
