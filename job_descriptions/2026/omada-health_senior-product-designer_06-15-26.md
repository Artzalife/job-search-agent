## Full Job Description

Omada Health is on a mission to bend the curve of chronic disease.

Job overview:

As a Senior Product Designer on Omada's new care programs and innovation team, you will work closely with product managers, engineers, behavioral researchers, and data scientists to explore, design, prototype, test, and iterate on solutions that help people make lasting changes to their health.

You are a human-centered designer at heart. You deeply understand and champion our users, designing thoughtful, elegant, and effective solutions grounded in real human needs. You thrive in ambiguity, especially in 0 to 1 spaces where the problem is not yet defined and the path forward is yours to shape. You bring a strong point of view grounded in user insight, product thinking, and craft.

Our tight-knit team of designers is obsessed with creating delightful, engaging, and inclusive programs for lifelong health, medical care, and behavior change. We combine behavioral psychology, data science, and user insight to create experiences that are intuitive and impactful across a wide range of users.

As a Senior Product Designer, you will…

Design human-centered experiences that help people make meaningful, lasting changes to their health.

Take on 0 to 1 problems, turning undefined spaces into clear concepts, prototypes, and shipped experiences.

Frame and define the right problems to solve, identifying opportunities across the member and care team experience.

Drive the design process end-to-end, from concept through validation, prototyping, and final execution, in fast, iterative cycles.

Use rapid prototyping to explore ideas, test hypotheses, and make concepts tangible for the team.

Deliver consumer-grade experiences across mobile (iOS and Android), with strong visual, interaction, and systems thinking.

Partner with product, engineering, clinical, and research teams to shape strategy and drive aligned decisions.

Translate business strategy, user needs, and clinical insights into compelling product opportunities and solutions.

Contribute to and improve design systems, processes, and team practices.

Design and ship human-centered AI experiences.

Use AI tools as part of your design workflow to explore ideas, prototype, synthesize research, generate concepts, or accelerate design production.

We'd love to talk more if you have…

6+ years of experience solving complex problems through product design, including 0 to 1 or early-stage product experience where you helped define new products or experiences from the ground up.

Experience designing for health, wellness, behavior change, or other complex human-centered domains.

A deep commitment to human-centered design, with a track record of grounding decisions in user research and real human needs.

Experience designing and shipping high-quality digital products on mobile (iOS and Android).

Strong product thinking, with the ability to frame ambiguous problems, define opportunities, and shape product direction.

High craft in interaction and visual design, with attention to detail and a strong sense of quality.

A strong bias toward making, using prototyping to explore ideas, test hypotheses, and drive decisions.

Experience working with and contributing to design systems.

Experience designing AI-powered products.

Experience using AI tools as part of your design workflow to explore ideas, prototype, and build, such as Figma Make, Claude Code, Cursor, or Lovable.

Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence decisions across teams.

Bonus points if you have…

Experience designing clinical, care delivery, or chronic condition management experiences.

Comfort running your own user research, including conducting interviews, synthesizing findings, and turning insights into design decisions.

You've worked in a startup setting and operate like a builder: self-driven, biased toward action, and energized by owning problems from discovery through launch.

Our Design Roots
Omada was founded in 2011 out of IDEO, where our founders explored how human-centered design could be applied to chronic health challenges. What began as an internal project grounded in deep user research evolved into a new approach to digital care.

That origin continues to shape how we work today. We place a strong emphasis on understanding real human needs, designing from first principles, and creating experiences that are not only effective, but engaging and thoughtfully crafted.

Design is not a layer at Omada. It is foundational to how we build products, make decisions, and deliver care.

About Omada Health: Omada Health (Nasdaq: OMDA) is reverse engineering the way healthcare is delivered in America, putting the space between doctor visits–where health is won or lost–at the center of care. Today's healthcare system poorly serves chronic conditions that require ongoing support outside of the exam room, like obesity, diabetes, hypertension, cholesterol, and musculoskeletal conditions. Omada’s virtual-first model combines human-led care teams, connected devices, and AI-enabled technology to deliver personalized care at scale, including support for GLP-1 therapy. Omada has served more than two million members since launch across 2,000+ employers, health plans, pharmacy benefit managers, and health systems. Learn more at omadahealth.com.
