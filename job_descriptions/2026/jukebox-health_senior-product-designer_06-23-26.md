## Full Job Description

At Jukebox Health, our mission is to empower everyone to live safer, healthier, more independent lives at home. We partner with health plans to make homes safer and more accessible for older adults and high-needs populations. We achieve this by combining technology with networks of clinicians, suppliers, and installers to deliver personalized home modifications and environmental supports nationwide. Founded by experienced entrepreneurs, Jukebox Health is a fast growing healthcare services company backed by top venture capital firms like Valtruis and The Home Depot.

We're looking for a Senior Product Designer with exceptional taste and a strong point of view to shape how clinicians and health plan partners experience Jukebox. You'll own design end-to-end, from early problem framing through polished, shippable UI, and set the bar for quality across our product. The ideal person has built tools that make complex workflows feel simple and beautiful.

Key Responsibilities

Own discovery through delivery for everything from internal to clinician-facing and partner-facing products

Translate ambiguous, complex workflows into intuitive, visually refined interfaces

Drive UX research and user testing to ground design decisions in real user needs

Build and maintain a coherent design system that scales across products

Collaborate closely with product and engineering to ship high-quality work, not just high-quality mocks

Raise the design bar across the organization by bringing a strong aesthetic sensibility and the conviction to defend it

Qualifications
Required

5+ years of product design experience with a portfolio demonstrating exceptional craft and taste

Deep expertise in Figma and modern prototyping workflows

Experience designing complex, workflow-heavy tools&nbsp;

Ability to operate independently end-to-end: research, information architecture, interaction design, visual design

Strong communicator who can articulate design rationale clearly to non-designers

Comfort with ambiguity and experience in a fast-moving startup environment with significant ownership and limited process overhead

Preferred

Experience working in healthcare with clinical, operational, or health plan workflows

Prior work on design systems at scale

Experience using AI to augment Product work
